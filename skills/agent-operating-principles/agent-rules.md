**When designing something new, or locking in a decision** → `agent-operating-principles`
- Survey prior art first, with extra weight for new architecture and load-bearing components (wire
  formats, encodings, protocols). Ask of each option: integrate it, lift the approach, or reuse its tests?
- Pre-commit decision criteria before the evidence exists, each able to kill a named option; record
  rejected options and their reasons as first-class results.
