**When filing a defect, or trusting a test or benchmark result** → `verification-instruments`
- Reproduce a defect against the real system before recording it — a plausible mechanism over-applied
  files a wrong claim.
- Two forms agreeing proves nothing when they share a convention; foreign vectors are what catch it.
- Mutation-check the fix, not the function. Run hand-applied mutations in an isolated worktree, never
  the main tree.
